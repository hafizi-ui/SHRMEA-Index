Step 1 · Guide

How this audit works

Each firm's corporate sustainability policy (waste reduction, energy efficiency, circular practices) is audited from two sides: the Employer (management, ER) view and the Employee (floor, EE) view.

Where the two views disagree is an audit finding — a perception gap showing where policy is not reaching the floor.

ER · Employer
Management's view of policy and practice.
EE · Employee
The floor's lived experience of that policy.

Workflow

  1. Data Entry — name each firm and choose A / P / X for ER and EE on every indicator. This is the only tab you edit.
  2. Scoring — A = Achieved (1), P = Partial (0.5), X = Not achieved (0).
  3. Comparison — ranks firms on total score, alignment %, maturity stage (1–7) and alignment gap.
  4. Spider Charts — ER vs EE web per firm, and cross-company overlays.
  5. Gap Analysis — signed gap (ER − EE) per indicator and firm, colour-coded.
  6. Four-Pillar Diagnosis — Knowledge, Incentive, Resource, Value; flags the “Fix First” pillar.
  7. Strategy & Action — apply the action matched to the flagged pillar.

The four gap pillars

Scoring key

  • AAchieved / Aligned — fully in place; intent & practice evidenced
  • PPartial — exists but inconsistent; policy–practice gap
  • XNot achieved — absent or clear contradiction
Tip: leave a firm blank if you assess fewer than 10 firms — blanks are ignored everywhere.
